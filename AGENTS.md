The first research priority is precisely the unpointed inclusion `AS_OU_3 ∩ I_3 ⊆ H_hyp_3`: decide whether an OU cubic-type category satisfying the intrinsic degree-three conditions must admit a standard AS4 cubic presentation. The current integral genus-one recognition theorem covers smooth, nodal and cuspidal curves with nine line bundles of degrees 0/1/2 and the standard OU noetherian qgr/derived helix comparison; it gives the marked hypersurface presentation under the intrinsic conditions, without blowup general position. AZ2 gives local freeness at every singular point of a reduced curve. For reduced reducible plane cubics with three orthogonal blocks of three line bundles of total degrees 0/1/2 and forward higher Ext vanishing, the geometric ample shift criterion is delta_md=A+C-2B=0 and w=5B-3A-2C positive in every component, by multidegree comparison of twists and finite enumeration. It includes transverse/tangent line-conic and triangle/concurrent lines. Under the standard OU comparison and I_3 the marked recognition follows. Under the intrinsic conditions and standard OU comparison, any geometric h is automatically ample (finite-length point-object argument). A nonzero component of the anticanonical natural transformation epsilon on the heart implies delta_md=0 even without a geometric h or exact J. For nodal Kodaira cycles C_2,C_3 with a very strong derived helix (no noetherian qgr comparison or t-exact s needed for the growth theorem), the punctured-torus model proves a finite power of the mapping class of h is a multitwist, total Hom growth to every fixed M is O(n), delta_md=0 and epsilon is nonzero on every helix object, without assuming I_3. The grading argument refines the mapping class to a multitwist with separating axes disjoint from the nine curves; if there are no separating axes the geometric conjugacy has nonnegative degrees. Simultaneous changes of all nine line-bundle curves by explicit mapping-class words, lifted via Opper, and exact free-group conjugacy certificates now recognize all nodal C_2 and C_3 presentations under the standard OU comparison and I_3. All 184 necessary multidegree patterns reduce to a positive geometric shift, an excluded zero-degree boundary, or a contraction. Counts: C_2 12 conditional recognition/4 excluded, C_3 90 conditional recognition/78 excluded. The complete recognition theorem does not depend on the graded multitwist refinement, mod-3 axis count, or the general ample-conjugacy theorem. A component-preserving deformation argument gives delta_md=0 for tangent line-conic and concurrent lines with a very strong derived helix as well, not nonvanishing of epsilon on the special fiber. Type III is now recognized under the standard comparison and I_3 by algebraic spherical-twist moves, rank-two evaluation, contraction using conductor first jets, and the classical reference lemma without ampleness. For type IV the algebraic moves, explicit first-jet composition comparison and periodic-object rank obstruction now give 90 conditional recognition/78 excluded/0 remaining among 168 necessary patterns. The 18 patterns with w=(-1,2,2) have the same directed algebras as positive w=(1,1,1) presentations for every allowed gluing parameter, not necessarily via curve autoequivalences. The last six patterns reduce to one explicit relation family (prop:ou-iv-residual-relation-family): p_{i,i+1,i}-p_{i,i+2,i}=0, p_{iik}-p_{ikk}+x_ik p_{ilk}=0 for i != k, l the remaining index, with x_01+x_12+x_20=x_02+x_21+x_10. The whole family is excluded from I_3 under the standard OU comparison by three s-fixed simple objects Q_i, arbitrarily long finite-length quotients T_m of a fixed helix object, and AZ2 plus the bounded Euler defect of an intrinsic marking. The linear constraint on x is needed for T_m, not for Q_i. This finishes the reduced case (thm:ou-reduced-complete-recognition). For nonreduced plane cubics, 3l has no degree-one line bundle; for 2l+m the forward Ext bounds and generation by adjacent arrows force the nine line bundles to be trivial on 2l after a common twist. Contract the double line to the cusp, descend the line bundles and preserve the directed algebra. The theorem thm:ou-all-plane-cubic-recognition now recognizes every plane cubic line-bundle presentation under the AOU three-block standard noetherian OU comparison and I_3. The new determinant-plane construction (prop:ou-full-support-determinant) uses the three matrices C_k entrywise multiplied by [[1,1,1],[1,a,b],[1,c,d]], giving three linear forms in z=[ad:bc:a:b:c:d] and z0*z3*z4=z1*z2*z5. Three independent forms and a smooth plane section suffice for the reconstruction of the nine bundles. For an integral section the criterion thm:ou-integral-determinant-criterion is necessary and sufficient for a labeled integral cubic line-bundle presentation: all nine even/odd coordinate-line intersections must be smooth and the bundle matrices must have rank two at all singular points. The cyclic quadratic relations and degree-three syzygy equations alpha_k c_ijk=beta_j c_i'jk give necessary support-graph conditions, and for full-support tensors exclude rank-one entrywise slice ratios. In full support with very strongness, determinant rank two in any cyclic cut is equivalent to the classical smooth cubic surface AOU foundation (cor:ou-dense-commutative-rank-criterion): the dual secant gives a smooth cubic surface and its nine grid lines and kernel line bundles identify the standard collection. Otherwise all cyclic ranks are three and no zero plane is contained in the binomial cubic. For rank three, a reduced curve presentation is recovered under the three coefficient tests (thm:ou-reduced-determinant-grid): independent restrictions of opposite parity coordinates to the plane, independent corresponding columns of the determinant coefficient matrix, and for each k some k' with det(C_k' / C_k) nonzero (entrywise division). The integral case gives a necessary and sufficient criterion; the reducible case is only sufficient. Reconstruction itself does not require very strongness. A numerical rotation example has a bad integral cut and two good reducible cuts, not a verified OU or I_3 example. For full-support tensors, thm:ou-dense-no-triple-torus-failure excludes simultaneous failure of (iii) in all three cyclic cuts using length-four/five syzygies.
