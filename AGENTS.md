The first research priority is precisely the unpointed inclusion `AS_OU_3 ∩ I_3 ⊆ H_hyp_3`: decide whether an OU cubic-type category satisfying the intrinsic degree-three conditions must admit a standard AS4 cubic presentation. The current integral genus-one recognition theorem covers smooth, nodal and cuspidal curves with nine line bundles of degrees 0/1/2 and the standard OU noetherian qgr/derived helix comparison; it gives the marked hypersurface presentation under the intrinsic conditions, without blowup general position. AZ2 gives local freeness at every singular point of a reduced curve. For reduced reducible plane cubics with three orthogonal blocks of three line bundles of total degrees 0/1/2 and forward higher Ext vanishing, the geometric ample shift criterion is delta_md=A+C-2B=0 and w=5B-3A-2C positive in every component, by multidegree comparison of twists and finite enumeration. It includes transverse/tangent line-conic and triangle/concurrent lines. Under the standard OU comparison and I_3 the marked recognition follows. Under the intrinsic conditions and standard OU comparison, any geometric h is automatically ample (finite-length point-object argument). A nonzero component of the anticanonical natural transformation epsilon on the heart implies delta_md=0 even without a geometric h or exact J. For nodal Kodaira cycles C_2,C_3 with a very strong derived helix (no noetherian qgr comparison or t-exact s needed for the growth theorem), the punctured-torus model proves a finite power of the mapping class of h is a multitwist, total Hom growth to every fixed M is O(n), delta_md=0 and epsilon is nonzero on every helix object, without assuming I_3. The grading argument refines the mapping class to a multitwist with separating axes disjoint from the nine curves; if there are no separating axes the geometric conjugacy has nonnegative degrees. Simultaneous changes of all nine line-bundle curves by explicit mapping-class words, lifted via Opper, and exact free-group conjugacy certificates now recognize all nodal C_2 and C_3 presentations under the standard OU comparison and I_3. The new determinant-plane construction (prop:ou-full-support-determinant) uses the three matrices C_k entrywise multiplied by [[1,1,1],[1,a,b],[1,c,d]], giving three linear forms in z=[ad:bc:a:b:c:d] and z0*z3*z4=z1*z2*z5. For an integral section the criterion thm:ou-integral-determinant-criterion is necessary and sufficient for a labeled integral cubic line-bundle presentation: all nine even/odd coordinate-line intersections must be smooth and the bundle matrices must have rank two at all singular points.
